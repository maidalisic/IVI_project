Source: YouGov survey, April 12–13, 2021, n=1,224 US adults, reconstructed from the published cross-tabulation PDF. Gender and region gaps are flagged significant when a two-proportion z-test / chi-square test survives a Holm correction across all 15 animals (α=.05). The dataset reports marginal breakdowns only (total, by gender, by age, by region computed independently) — not a full cross-tabulation, so combined effects (e.g. young men specifically) cannot be shown. Correlations in the Explore tab beyond the pre-registered danger×age pair are computed live (Pearson r) across n=15 animals and are illustrative, not hypothesis-tested.
